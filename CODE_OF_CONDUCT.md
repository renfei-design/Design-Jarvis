# Code of conduct

Design Jarvis is committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and generous in discussion and review.
- Critique ideas and artifacts rather than people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds, abilities, identities, and levels of experience.
- Protect private information and obtain consent before sharing another person’s work or statements.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using the repository’s private reporting channel. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect confidentiality as far as practical, and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, free from conflicts of interest, and open to reconsideration when material new information is available.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, community events, and other spaces where someone is acting on behalf of Design Jarvis.
