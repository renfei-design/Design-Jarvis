# Punctuation

Rules for consistent punctuation in UI and documentation.

## Periods

- **End sentences with periods** — Including the last sentence in body text
- **No periods in headings** — Unless it's a full sentence in running text
- **No periods in button labels** — "Save changes" not "Save changes."
- **No periods in tooltips** — Unless multiple sentences

## Commas

- **Use serial comma** (Oxford comma): "pipelines, dataflows, and notebooks"
- **Use commas for clarity** in complex sentences
- **Don't use comma** before "that" in restrictive clauses

## Colons

- **Introduce lists**: "The following items:"
- **No colon in headings** that introduce immediate content

### Capitalization after colons

| What follows | Capitalization | Example |
|--------------|----------------|----------|
| Complete sentence | Capitalize | "The feature offers one benefit: It reduces latency by 50%." |
| Fragment or phrase | Lowercase | "The feature offers one benefit: reduced latency." |
| List | Lowercase | "Choose from three options: basic, standard, or premium." |
| Proper noun | Capitalize | "The tool integrates with one platform: the product." |

## Apostrophes

- **Possessives**: "the user's settings"
- **Contractions**: "don't", "it's" (it is), "you're"
- **Not for plurals**: ✅ "APIs" ❌ "API's"

## Quotation Marks

- **Periods and commas inside** quotes (US style)
- **Use for UI text** being referenced: Select "Save"
- **Use for unfamiliar terms** on first use

## Hyphens and Dashes

- **Hyphen (-)**: Compound modifiers: "real-time data", "user-friendly"
- **En dash (–)**: Ranges: "pages 10–20"
- **Em dash (—)**: Use sparingly for emphasis or interruption

## Slashes

- Avoid when possible; use "or" instead
- ✅ "Select Monday or Tuesday"
- ❌ "Select Monday/Tuesday"

## Exclamation Points

- Use sparingly
- Never more than one
- Reserve for genuine excitement: "Welcome to the product!"
- Never in error messages

## Common Fixes

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "APIs, SDK's, and tools" | "APIs, SDKs, and tools" |
| "Click Save." (button) | "Select Save" (no period) |
| "its" (possessive) vs "it's" (it is) | Check context |
| "Monday/Tuesday" | "Monday or Tuesday" |
